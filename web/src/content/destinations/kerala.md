Most of our Kerala trips start in Kochi and end there, with the hills of Munnar and a houseboat night in between. Families often add a beach stay at Varkala or Kovalam; couples tend to want a quieter houseboat and a resort with a view.

The monsoon (June to September) brings lower hotel prices and green hills, but some days are wet. Tell us your dates and we will say what to expect.
